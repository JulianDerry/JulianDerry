## Evidence Standard

Every substantial investigation should document, where applicable:

1. Case objective
2. Evidence source
3. Acquisition method
4. Hash values and integrity verification
5. Examination methodology
6. Relevant artifacts
7. Timeline reconstruction
8. Correlation and validation
9. Findings
10. Limitations
11. Conclusion
12. Screenshots and supporting evidence

Training, laboratory, synthetic and authorized evidence must be identified accurately. No portfolio entry should imply real-world casework when it was not.

## Portfolio Principle

The portfolio is optimized for evidence of analyst capability, not project count. New projects should add a distinct forensic capability, investigative question, engineering capability or artifact-level research area.
