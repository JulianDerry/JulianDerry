## Forensic Methodology

My workflow keeps a clear distinction between what an artifact contains and what it can actually prove.

```text
Identification
      ↓
Preservation
      ↓
Acquisition
      ↓
Hash Verification
      ↓
Examination
      ↓
Analysis
      ↓
Correlation
      ↓
Timeline Reconstruction
      ↓
Reporting
```

Where automated output is used, important findings are manually checked against the underlying evidence whenever possible.

- Timeline reconstruction
- Correlation of findings
- What the evidence established
- What the evidence did not establish
- Limitations and considerations
- Professional forensic reporting

All portfolio case studies are clearly identified as training work, laboratory work, or authorized evidence where applicable.
