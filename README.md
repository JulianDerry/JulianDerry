## What I do

I reconstruct what happened on disk, in memory, on a phone, in email, on removable media, or in a vehicle's infotainment system, and turn digital artifacts into defensible findings.

My work covers acquisition, evidence integrity, artifact examination, analysis, correlation, timeline reconstruction, detection, and reporting.

```text
acquire evidence → verify integrity → analyze artifacts → correlate evidence → reconstruct timeline → report findings

```
